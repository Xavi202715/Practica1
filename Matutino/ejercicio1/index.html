Pupusas

Pupusas are a traditional dish from El Salvador, with origins tracing back to the Pipil tribes, and are considered the national dish of the country.

Rigua

Riguas are not only a staple in Salvadoran cuisine but also a symbol of the country's agricultural heritage and cultural significance.

Empanadas

The history of empanadas in El Salvador is deeply rooted in the country's culinary traditions and the creative use of local ingredients.
